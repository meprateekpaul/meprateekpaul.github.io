Pedagogical Design

The Central Molecular Thread: Human Beta-Globin (*HBB*)

Instead of disconnected toy examples, the entire semester investigates human beta-globin (NM_000518, NP_000509 / P68871, PDB 1A3N/4HHB), the sickle-cell variant (E6V, 2HBS), and the vertebrate globin family across all units.

Database Flat File

Parse NM_000518 line-by-line: LOCUS, CDS coordinates (51..494), translation, splice sites, and polyA signal.

Alignment & BLAST

Align human HBB against myoglobin with BLOSUM62 and PAM matrices; evaluate bit scores, E-values, and identify the HbS Glu6Val mutation.

MSA & Phylogeny

Construct 7-globin MSA in Clustal Omega; visualize catalytic proximal & distal histidines in Jalview; build UPGMA and NJ trees.

3D Structure & Sickle Cell

Examine PDB 1A3N and 2HBS in VMD and Mol*; visualize the hydrophobic patch created by Val6 causing polymer fiber aggregation.

Semester Calibration

The 4-Month (15-Week) Mastery Roadmap

Calibrated for 8–10 hours per week of total student effort (~140 hours total). Designed to build self-reliance, conceptual rigor, and computational confidence from scratch.

Month 1 · Weeks 1–4

Data Literacy & Repositories

Linux CLI navigation & pipes; NCBI Entrez system; UID and RefSeq prefixes; the NCBI ASN.1 data model; line-by-line GenBank flat file anatomy.

Milestone: Quiz 1 (Units 1–3)
Month 2 · Weeks 5–8

The Algorithmic Core

Dynamic programming by hand: Needleman–Wunsch global & Smith–Waterman local alignment; PAM & BLOSUM log-odds matrices; Karlin–Altschul statistics; BLAST & FASTA heuristics.

Milestone: Mid-Semester Exam
Month 3 · Weeks 9–12

Evolution & Sequence Prediction

Progressive MSA (Clustal Omega, Jalview); phylogenetic trees (orthologues vs. paralogues, UPGMA, NJ, bootstrap); ORFs & codon bias; ProtParam, PROSITE motifs, GOR IV/PSIPRED secondary structure.

Milestone: Assignment 1 Due
Month 4 · Weeks 13–15

3D Structures & Capstone

Macromolecular PDB files (ATOM, HETATM); 3D rendering in VMD and Mol*; structural similarity & homology modeling; comparative genomics; independent gene family capstone project.

Milestone: End-Sem Exam & Viva

Core University Textbooks

  • Baxevanis, A. D., Bader, G. D., & Wishart, D. S. (Eds.) (2020). Bioinformatics: A Practical Guide to the Analysis of Genes and Proteins (4th ed.). John Wiley & Sons. ISBN: 978-1-119-54744-0. Primary unit framework; ASN.1 & NCBI Data Model detailed in 2nd ed. (2001).
  • Mount, D. W. (2004). Bioinformatics: Sequence and Genome Analysis (2nd ed.). Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory Press. ISBN: 978-0-87969-712-9. Definitive reference for dynamic programming (NW/SW), PAM/BLOSUM log-odds, and E-values.
  • Lesk, A. M. (2019). Introduction to Bioinformatics (5th ed.). Oxford University Press. ISBN: 978-0-19-879414-1. Core text for protein structural hierarchy, evolutionary divergence, and tree interpretation.
  • Pevsner, J. (2015). Bioinformatics and Functional Genomics (3rd ed.). Wiley-Blackwell. ISBN: 978-1-118-58178-0. Practical guide for GenBank flat files, PSI-BLAST profiles, and comparative genomics.
