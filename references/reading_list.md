# Concepts of Bioinformatics — Reading List & Bibliography

> **Course:** CBBI-501 · B.Tech. Bioinformatics, Semester II

---

## Primary Course Text

- **Baxevanis, A. D., Bader, G. D., & Wishart, D. S. (Eds.). (2020).**  
  *Bioinformatics: A Practical Guide to the Analysis of Genes and Proteins* (4th ed.). John Wiley & Sons.  
  *Note:* The syllabus unit topics directly align with Baxevanis & Ouellette. For detailed historical background on ASN.1 and the NCBI data model, consult Chapters 1 & 2 of the 2nd Edition (2001).

---

## Supporting & Supplementary Textbooks

1. **Mount, D. W. (2004).**  
   *Bioinformatics: Sequence and Genome Analysis* (2nd ed.). Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory Press.  
   *(Essential reference for Dynamic Programming, Needleman–Wunsch, Smith–Waterman, PAM/BLOSUM derivations, and statistical significance of E-values).*

2. **Lesk, A. M. (2019).**  
   *Introduction to Bioinformatics* (5th ed.). Oxford University Press.  
   *(Superb treatment of protein structural hierarchy, evolutionary divergence, and phylogenetic tree construction).*

3. **Pevsner, J. (2015).**  
   *Bioinformatics and Functional Genomics* (3rd ed.). Wiley-Blackwell.  
   *(In-depth, practical walkthroughs of NCBI BLAST, GenBank flat files, PSI-BLAST profiles, and comparative genomics).*

4. **Xiong, J. (2006).**  
   *Essential Bioinformatics.* Cambridge University Press.  
   *(Concise, accessible introduction designed specifically for first-year undergraduate trainees).*

## Key Milestone Papers (Classic Literature)

- **Dayhoff, M. O., Schwartz, R. M., & Orcutt, B. C. (1978).** A model of evolutionary change in proteins. In M. O. Dayhoff (Ed.), *Atlas of Protein Sequence and Structure* (Vol. 5, Suppl. 3, pp. 345–352). National Biomedical Research Foundation.

- **Needleman, S. B., & Wunsch, C. D. (1970).** A general method applicable to the search for similarities in the amino acid sequence of two proteins. *Journal of Molecular Biology*, 48(3), 443-453. https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-2836(70)90057-4
- **Chou, P. Y., & Fasman, G. D. (1974).** Prediction of protein conformation. *Biochemistry*, 13(2), 211–222. https://doi.org/10.1021/bi00699a001
- **Smith, T. F., & Waterman, M. S. (1981).** Identification of common molecular subsequences. *Journal of Molecular Biology*, 147(1), 195-197. https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-2836(81)90087-5
- **Kabsch, W., & Sander, C. (1983).** Dictionary of protein secondary structure: pattern recognition of hydrogen-bonded and geometrical features. *Biopolymers*, 22(12), 2577–2637. https://doi.org/10.1002/bip.360221211
- **Saitou, N., & Nei, M. (1987).** The neighbor-joining method: a new method for reconstructing phylogenetic trees. *Molecular Biology and Evolution*, 4(4), 406–425. https://doi.org/10.1093/oxfordjournals.molbev.a040454
